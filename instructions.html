Rules & Guidelines

  • Timer Countdown: The exam timer starts immediately after clicking "Start Exam". It will automatically submit your attempt when time reaches zero.
  • Question Palette: You can navigate freely between questions using the Question Palette or Next/Previous controls.
  • Mark for Review: You can mark questions for review if you wish to revisit them before final submission.
  • Browser & Tab Security: Switching browser tabs, minimizing the browser, or exiting fullscreen mode will trigger a security log warning.
  • State Auto-Save: If you accidentally refresh your page, your answered responses and remaining time will be preserved.
Anti-Cheating Notice:
Browser monitoring is active for demo purposes. Full proctoring requires backend servers and webcam monitoring.
